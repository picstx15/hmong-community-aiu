
# Hmong Community at AIU

## About the Project

Hmong Community at AIU is a bilingual website project designed to promote Hmong culture and traditions, introduce the Hmong youth community at Asia-Pacific International University (AIU), and preserve memories of the annual Hmong New Year celebration.

## Project Objectives

- Promote Hmong culture and traditions.
- Introduce the Hmong youth community at AIU.
- Preserve memories of Hmong New Year celebrations.
- Share cultural knowledge in Thai and English.

## Target Users

- Hmong students at AIU.
- AIU students and staff.
- People interested in Hmong culture and traditions.

## Technology Stack

- HTML5
- Tailwind CSS
- JavaScript
